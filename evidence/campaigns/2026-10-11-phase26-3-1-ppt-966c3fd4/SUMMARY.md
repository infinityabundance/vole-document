# Phase 26.3.1 — legacy PowerPoint 97–2003 (`.ppt`, MS-PPT) court

**Question.** Does a legacy PowerPoint 97–2003 presentation close exactly and
expose a representation-preserving semantic model (the MS-PPT record stream with
each record's recVer/recInstance/recType/recLen, exact span, stream offset, and
nesting depth, the `Document` container, the `SlideListWithText` slides with their
`persistIdRef`, and the `TextCharsAtom` (UTF-16LE) / `TextBytesAtom` (CP1252) runs
with their exact spans and decoded text) **on top of the reused CFBF/OLE2 physical
layer**, while staying honest at the detection boundary and declining malformed
inputs typed?

**Method.** Each fixture is generated by the dependency-free
`examples/ppt_fixtures.rs` writer, ingested via `field-build`, observed, then —
after the **source file and the standalone descriptor are deleted** —
rematerialized exactly in a fresh process and compared with `length + SHA-256 +
cmp`. The CFBF, Opaque, and structural controls pin the detection boundaries. The
court runs in the pinned `dev` service using only POSIX `sh`, coreutils, git, and
the shipped binary (no python3, no PowerPoint tooling, no network).

## Scope (honest)

- **Supported:** detection on top of the CFBF layer (the `PowerPoint Document`
  stream begins with a `Document` record — `recVer 0xF`, `recType 1000` — whose
  header is `0F 00 E8 03`); the record stream (the 8-byte `RecordHeader` with
  `recVer = w & 0x0F`, `recInstance = (w >> 4) & 0x0FFF`, `recType`, `recLen`) with
  every record's exact byte span, stream offset, and nesting depth; the
  `SlideListWithText` slides (`persistIdRef`, in order, for the slides list); the
  `TextCharsAtom` (UTF-16LE) and `TextBytesAtom` (CP1252) runs with their exact
  spans and decoded text; the `Current User` stream's `UserEditAtom` offset when it
  resolves; native `ppt-document`/`ppt-slide`/`ppt-run`/`ppt-record`/`ppt-find`;
  common `metadata`/`text`/`search-match`.
- **Boundary:** a generic compound file, and a `PowerPoint Document` stream whose
  first record is not a `Document`, stay `Cfbf`; prose stays `Opaque`; a `.pptx`
  (an OPC ZIP) never reaches the CFBF detector at all; a `.ppt` with a plausible
  `Document` record but a malformed body is admitted and its model declines typed.
- **Declines:** a `Document` container whose declared `recLen` is smaller than its
  children (a child overruns its container), a `recLen` that overruns the stream, a
  truncated record header, an odd-length `TextCharsAtom` (a text-atom/`recLen`
  mismatch), a text atom with no preceding `SlidePersistAtom`, and an out-of-range
  `Current User` `UserEditAtom` offset are typed declines
  (`InvalidPptStructure`).
- **Not claimed here:** the `Current User` stream's *content* beyond the
  `UserEditAtom` offset, the master/notes `SlideListWithText` lists (only the
  slides list is modeled), embedded OLE objects, pictures, and the other legacy
  CFBF semantic models (MSG, later subphases); the economic court (separate script).
- **Exactness** is the whole source (a RAW-like `DocumentExact`): the PPT model
  is derived and never on the exactness path (ADR-0060: the model node depends on
  the `sha256(source)` root).
- **Never run on the host:** every command above ran in the pinned `dev`
  container.
