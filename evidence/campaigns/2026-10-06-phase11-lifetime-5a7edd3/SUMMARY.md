# Phase 11 lifetime-cost court — results

Generated by `tools/field-lifetime-court.sh` inside the pinned `db-baseline` service.
Measures the **cumulative lifetime cost** of answering N page-text queries for
N ∈ {1 10 100 1000} for VOLE (Phase 11), A0 (raw `pdftotext`), and A1 (preprocessed SQLite),
each with its one-time cost charged in full, then reports the crossover (or its absence).
A "pass" answers the schedule once in a fresh process per query; pass 1 is cold and the
final pass is warm. CPU is an aggregate batch measurement (`/usr/bin/time -v`); a single
sub-millisecond query is below its 10 ms resolution. VOLE additionally answers a 5-surface
pre-registered set (text, structure, preview, object bytes, decoded stream), reported for
VOLE alone because the conventional baselines cannot answer those surfaces.

Note on definitions: VOLE `total_bytes_read` is the instrumented `ObserveStats.bytes_read` (descriptor+manifest+index+seed); A0/A1 `total_bytes_read` is the process `read`/`pread64` total under strace, which also includes library reads. The two are not the same measurement and are never summed together. CPU is an aggregate batch measurement.
