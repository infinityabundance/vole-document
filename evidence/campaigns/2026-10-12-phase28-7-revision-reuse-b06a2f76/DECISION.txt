DECISION
  criterion: retained>=20% [yes]  index<=10% of L1 [yes]  graph delta < CAS byte saving [yes]
  RESOLVED (positive, scoped, marginal): the minimal dependency-signature
  index does NOT consume the byte advantage. A fragment-level physical
  content-addressed store already captures the byte reuse (12,634,084 B saved vs a
  full rebuild, 36.5% of L1 raw), and the graph adds only 3,115,552 B of index
  (9.1% of L1 raw) while reusing 83.4% of next-revision verified
  observations. The semantic edges are load-bearing: 986 invalidations have
  UNCHANGED physical bytes and would be reused WRONG by a physical-only store.
  FRAGILITY (stated, not hidden): the verdict is marginal on the 10%
  index criterion (9.1%). If the design also persists a 48 B per-observation
  identity node the index becomes 12,289,312 B (35.5%) and the verdict flips to
  NEGATIVE. SCOPE: the work win lives in LOCALIZED edits; on a whole-document
  redraft it is 0 by construction (and the byte baseline there is also ~0).
