# Phase 27.2.1 — legacy PowerPoint 97–2003 (`.ppt`, MS-PPT) list court

**Question.** Does every `SlideListWithText` container — the **slides** (0),
**masters** (1), **notes** (2), and **handout** (3) lists — close exactly and
materialize as its own typed, span-preserving answer (the exact container span, the
ordered `SlidePersistAtom` entries, and for each the ordered `TextCharsAtom`
(UTF-16LE) / `TextBytesAtom` (CP1252) runs with their exact spans and decoded
text), while the slides-list answers stay byte-for-byte unchanged and malformed
inputs (including a malformed auxiliary list) decline typed?

**Method.** Each fixture is generated by the dependency-free
`examples/ppt_fixtures.rs` writer, ingested via `field-build`, observed, then —
after the **source file and the standalone descriptor are deleted** —
rematerialized exactly in a fresh process and compared with `length + SHA-256 +
cmp`. The CFBF, Opaque, and structural controls pin the detection boundaries. The
court runs in the pinned `dev` service using only POSIX `sh`, coreutils, git, and
the shipped binary (no python3, no PowerPoint tooling, no network).

## Scope (honest)

- **Supported:** the `SlideListWithText` lists `slides`/`masters`/`notes`/
  `handout` (by `recInstance`) with their ordered `SlidePersistAtom` entries and
  text runs, each with its exact span and decoded text; the exact container span;
  native `ppt-list`; the unchanged `ppt-slide`/`ppt-run`/`ppt-record`/`ppt-find`;
  common `metadata`/`text`/`search-match`.
- **Empty:** an absent list and a present-but-empty list are typed empty answers
  (`slides`:0, `runs`:0, `empty`:true), never errors.
- **Boundary:** a generic compound file, and a `PowerPoint Document` stream whose
  first record is not a `Document`, stay `Cfbf`; prose stays `Opaque`; a `.pptx`
  (an OPC ZIP) never reaches the CFBF detector at all; a `.ppt` with a plausible
  `Document` record but a malformed body is admitted and its model declines typed.
- **Declines:** a malformed body — including a malformed **auxiliary** list
  (an odd-length `TextCharsAtom` in a notes list), which the previous subphase
  silently skipped — is a typed decline (`InvalidPptStructure`).
- **Not claimed here:** the `Current User` stream's *content* beyond the
  `UserEditAtom` offset, embedded OLE objects, pictures, and the other legacy CFBF
  semantic models; the economic court (separate script).
- **Exactness** is the whole source (a RAW-like `DocumentExact`): the PPT model
  is derived and never on the exactness path (ADR-0060: the model node depends on
  the `sha256(source)` root).
- **Never run on the host:** every command above ran in the pinned `dev`
  container.
