## Honest caveats

* The partial lane requires an observation index; a descriptor without one falls
  back to the full blob, labelled `full` in `descriptor_read_mode`.
* A page observation reads its referenced channel(s) plus the CRC-checked
  GRAPH/INDEX records, so it is closure-bounded but not tiny; the producer
  document is small enough that its closure is nearly the whole blob.
* The partial read verifies each `record`'s CRC32C but does not re-hash the whole
  blob against its content id (that would read the whole file). A corrupted
  *unneeded* record is therefore not detected by a narrow observation.
* `TextMatch` still walks pages; the closure bound is per-page.
