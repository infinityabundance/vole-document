**Revision note.** This receipt measures the Phase-11.12 review fix #2: a *narrow* observation
no longer reads the whole `.voldoc`. `FieldStore::ingest_pdf` now adds an advisory
`OBSERVATION_INDEX` op table when the descriptor lacks one, and the observation path opens a
seek-based **partial** loader that walks only the record framing, reading the small records
(`UNIVERSE`/`GRAPH`/`INTEGRITY`/`TRAILER`/index) and only the `OBJECT`/`ENTROPY_CHANNEL`/
`MODEL` records a selected op references. `descriptor_bytes_read` is now closure-bounded and
tagged with `descriptor_read_mode` (`partial`|`full`); a descriptor without an index falls back
to the full parse and is labelled `full`. `materialize_exact` still reads and verifies the
whole source (the archival path). Every case still cross-checks the parsed `observe` `stats`
against `explain --analyze`. The prior receipts `2026-10-06-phase11-63f43fb` and
`2026-10-06-phase11-io-2e0a840` are retained unchanged for comparison.
