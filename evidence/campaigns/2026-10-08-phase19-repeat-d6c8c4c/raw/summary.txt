# Phase 19.1 — repeatability court (paired, interleaved, N=10)

Replaces the two single-shot Phase-18.5 point estimates with paired, interleaved, repeated measurements and a bootstrap interval. Same 12-document subset, same contract depths C0–C5, same source-retaining SQLite lane (`tools/fixtures/phase18-contract-packed.py`, byte-identical), same accounting (persistent bytes = sum of regular-file sizes; never `du -sb`). Wall times are integer microseconds.

- documents: **12** (nist-docx-0005, nist-docx-0008, nist-docx-0009, nist-docx-0014, nist-epub-0003, nist-epub-0006, nist-epub-0008, nist-epub-0009, nist-pdf-0002, nist-pdf-0004, nist-pdf-0016, nist-pdf-0017); depths: **C0..C5**; repetitions: **N=10**; bootstrap: **20000 resamples, seed 190019**, cluster-resampled by document; tie band **+/-10%**.
- interleaving: odd reps VOLE-then-SQLite, even reps SQLite-then-VOLE (per-rep order in `raw/order.tsv`); a per-rep warm-up precedes each lane's timed queries so the first read of a freshly rewritten store is not timed.
- every individual sample is retained in `raw/*_samples.tsv`; nothing is reduced to min/median at collection time.

## Answers

**Q1 — is the VOLE/SQLite BUILD ratio < 1.0?**

- Paired median ratio **0.182** (95% CI 0.102..0.228); geometric mean **0.203** (95% CI 0.128..0.395). Per-document: 11 win / 0 tie / 1 loss.
- The 95% CI lies **entirely below 1.0**: on this subset VOLE builds the equal contract faster than SQLite, and the effect is not within parity at these depths.

**Q2 — is the VOLE/SQLite WARM heterogeneous-session ratio > 1.0, or parity?**

- Paired median ratio **1.077** (95% CI 0.809..1.391); geometric mean **1.065** (95% CI 0.880..1.287). Per-document: 5 win / 1 tie / 6 loss.
- The 95% CI **includes 1.0**: warm latency is **indistinguishable from parity** at this sample size on this store and host — the Phase-18 1.09x is a point estimate, not a resolved loss.

## Caveats and honesty

- The bind-mounted host store is noisy; the per-document CVs in `raw/per_doc_lane_depth.csv` quantify that noise and are the reason the intervals are quoted at all. Wide intervals are reported as wide, not massaged.
- Warm sessions run in the low-millisecond range, where process start-up is a material fraction of the wall; that start-up is part of BOTH lanes' measured cost and is not subtracted.
- The previous 0.82x / 1.09x came from one run with a min-of-3 reduction; the min-of-3 vs median-of-10 rows above show how much that reduction moved each lane.
- Sampling unit for the CI is the document (cluster bootstrap), not the doc-rep pair; a document's 10 repeated measures are correlated and are not treated as 10 independent documents.
- recorded interleave orders: sqlite vole; vole sqlite.
